Privacy

We collect as little as possible.

The best way to protect your data is not to hold it. RepairOrReplace is built around data minimization: for an anonymous calculation, nothing personal is stored at all.

This page explains our practices in plain language; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

Principles

1

Data minimization

We only collect what a feature genuinely needs. Anonymous calculations need no personal data, so we keep none.

2

Purpose limitation

Data collected for one purpose (say, refreshing a saved estimate) isn’t repurposed for something you didn’t agree to.

3

No sale of personal information

We do not sell your personal information. If you request a quote from a local pro, only the details needed for that referral are shared, and only with your action.

4

Theme & local storage

Your light/dark preference is stored in your browser’s local storage on your device. It never reaches our servers.

Your rights (CCPA & GDPR)

Because we don’t retain personal data for anonymous use, most rights requests are simple. For saved accounts, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data associated with your account.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and its data (right to erasure).
  • Port your saved inventory to yourself in a machine-readable form.
  • Opt out of any non-essential communications at any time.

Under the CCPA you may exercise these rights without discrimination in the service you receive. Under the GDPR, our lawful bases are your consent (for a saved account and optional notifications) and legitimate interest (for running a calculation you asked for).

Cookies & analytics

We keep tracking to a minimum. We do not require third-party advertising cookies to deliver a calculation, and any analytics we use are configured to avoid collecting more than aggregate, privacy- respecting usage signals.

Questions or a rights request?

Reach us at privacy@repair-or-replace.net. We’ll respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.
