Our mission

The honest answer to one expensive question.

When a major appliance breaks, you get a repair quote and a nagging feeling you’re being steered. RepairOrReplace exists to give you the neutral, numbers-first answer — the one a disinterested engineer would give a friend.

Why we built this

“Repair or replace?” is one of the most common — and most lopsided — decisions in a household. On one side sits a repair technician who is paid to repair, and a retailer paid to sell you something new. On the other side sits you, armed with a rule of thumb from a search result and a quote you can’t easily sanity-check. The information is asymmetric, and the stakes are hundreds to thousands of dollars.

We wanted a tool with no dog in the fight: one that computes the economically rational answer from public data, tells you how confident it is, and warns you when a quote looks predatory — even if that means telling you to walk away from a sale.

What we stand for

1

Neutral by construction

The verdict is computed first and independently. Any affiliate or lead-gen link is appended afterward, in a physically separate block, and is never allowed to change the recommendation.

2

Transparent about the math

We publish the actual model and every data source. Read how it works and the methodology — nothing is a black box.

3

No lead-capture wall

You never have to hand over an email to see your answer. The calculator runs, shows the result, and asks for nothing in return.

4

Honest about uncertainty

When the inputs are thin or a quote is implausible, we say “we’re not sure” instead of faking a confident answer. See our no-fake-precision principle.

How we keep the lights on

We’re free to use because low-intent editorial pages may carry display advertising, and because we may earn a commission on parts or a referral fee if you choose to request a quote from a vetted local pro. Those placements are disclosed, and — this is the important part — they appear only after your verdict is calculated and never influence it. The financial result screen itself stays free of display ads so the recommendation is never obscured.

A tool, not a diagnosis

RepairOrReplace produces economic estimates to guide a decision. It is not a technical diagnosis or a safety certification — for anything involving gas, high voltage, or refrigerant, we’ll always route you to a licensed professional. See the terms.
