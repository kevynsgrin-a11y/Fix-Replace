Dishwasher guide

Dishwasher: repair or replace?

Dishwashers are the friendliest appliance to repair: nearly every common fault is a modestly priced, homeowner-serviceable part. The catch is a high install cost when you do replace — and the occasional wildly inflated quote.

Data reviewed July 19, 2026 · BLS OEWS 49-9031 · EIA residential rates

Because a dishwasher is plumbed and wired into the cabinetry, replacing one carries roughly $440 of delivery, installation, and haul-away on top of the unit price. That, plus the fact that most repairs are cheap and DIY-friendly, means the scales usually tip toward fixing — right up until the unit ages out of its lifespan band.

The classic predatory quote

A drain-pump replacement runs about $150–$320 installed. If you’re quoted $900 for that job, it’s more than double the top of the band — the exact pattern our model flags as likely predatory. Energy is a small factor

A modern dishwasher uses about 240 kWh/year versus 330 kWh/year for an aging one — roughly a 90 kWh saving, or about $16/year nationally. Helpful over a long horizon, but rarely the deciding factor.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a dishwasher last?

A dishwasher lasts about 6–9 years at the budget tier, 9–12 years mid-range, and 15–20 years premium. Mid-range is the most common band for a typical household unit.

Is it worth repairing a dishwasher?

Usually yes, because most dishwasher faults are DIY-friendly and modestly priced: a drain pump is $150–$320, a control board $200–$500, a door seal $100–$250, and a water inlet valve $120–$250. Repair generally wins while the unit is inside its lifespan band; a $400+ professional repair on a 10-plus-year budget unit is where replacement starts to make sense.

A shop quoted $900 to fix my dishwasher — is that fair?

For a common repair like a drain pump, $900 is far above the typical $150–$320 installed range — more than double the top of the band. That’s the exact case our model flags as potentially predatory and withholds a verdict on, advising a second quote before you authorize the work.

How much does it cost to replace a dishwasher?

A new dishwasher is about $450 budget, $800 mid-range, or $1,500 premium, plus roughly $440 in delivery, installation, and haul-away, because dishwashers are plumbed and wired in rather than plugged in.
