A “high” Boston quote may still be fair

Because Boston’s labor rate is roughly a quarter above the national average, out-of-town price charts will make fair local quotes look inflated. Our predatory-quote guard uses the Boston band, so it won’t wrongly flag a fair local price — and it will still catch a quote that’s genuinely double the regional norm. High electricity rates (~31 ¢/kWh) also nudge energy-hungry appliances toward replacement.
