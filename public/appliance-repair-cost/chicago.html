Frequently asked questions

How much does appliance repair cost in Chicago?

Chicago bands run $104–$197 for a dryer thermal fuse, $123–$288 for an oven or range bake element, $155–$330 for a dishwasher or washer drain pump, $259–$466 for a washer drum bearing and $512–$1,281 for a refrigerator sealed-system leak. Each applies Chicago’s ≈1.05× labor multiplier to the labor share only.

Why is Chicago a good stand-in for national appliance repair prices?

Its wage is almost the national one. The BLS area mean for appliance repairers (49-9031) in Chicago is $25.30/hr against a $24.10 national mean — about 5% above, or ≈1.05×. Because that multiplier only touches the labor share, Chicago’s bands sit within a few percent of the nationwide figures in our cost guides.

How do I tell whether a Chicago repair quote is out of line?

Compare it to the Chicago band for that exact fault, not to a whole-appliance average. A $600 quote against the $259–$466 washer drum bearing band is high but arguable; $1,000 for the same job is more than double the band, which is where our predatory-quote guard flags it. A $512–$1,281 sealed-system quote, by contrast, is simply an expensive repair.

With Illinois electricity around 16 ¢/kWh, does energy favor repairing in Chicago?

Modestly, yes. At about 16 ¢/kWh, a refrigerator drawing roughly 400 kWh a year more than a modern one costs around $64/year extra to run — a little under the roughly $71 national figure, and well under high-rate metros. With energy pressure that mild, the call here is decided mainly by the repair price and the unit’s age.
