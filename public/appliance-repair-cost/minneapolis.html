Minneapolis vs. Miami: same repair, different fair price

Minneapolis labor ($28.51/hr) runs well above Miami’s ($22.87/hr), so the identical repair is genuinely pricier here — not a sign of gouging. And with Minnesota electricity around 15 ¢/kWh, an old appliance costs less to run than in a high-rate state, which can tilt a close call back toward repairing rather than replacing.
