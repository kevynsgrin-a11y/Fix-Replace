New York’s energy rate shifts the decision

At 26.49 ¢/kWh — roughly 49% above the national average — running an aging appliance costs more here than almost anywhere else. A worn refrigerator that wastes ~400 kWh/year over a modern one costs about $106/year extra in New York (versus ~$71 nationally), which shortens the payback on replacing and can flip a marginal repair-vs-replace call toward buying new.
