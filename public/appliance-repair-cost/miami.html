Frequently asked questions

How much does appliance repair cost in Miami?

Miami carries the lowest bands we publish: $96–$183 for a dryer thermal fuse, $117–$272 for an oven or range bake element, $174–$271 for a refrigerator evaporator fan motor, $241–$434 for a washer drum bearing and $487–$1,218 for a refrigerator sealed-system leak — all at Miami’s ≈0.95× labor multiplier on the labor share.

Why is appliance repair cheaper in Miami than in most metros?

The BLS area mean wage for appliance repairers (49-9031) in Miami is $22.87/hr, below the $24.10 national mean, giving a ≈0.95× multiplier — the only one under 1.00 among our indexed metros, and roughly 23% under Boston’s $29.60/hr. Parts are still national, so the saving lands on labor-heavy jobs like the $338–$532 washer transmission.

Can a quote that looks normal elsewhere still be too high in Miami?

Yes, and that’s the trap. A $520 washer drum bearing would be unremarkable in a high-wage metro but sits above the $241–$434 Miami band. Judging local quotes against a national chart hides that gap, which is why our predatory-quote guard scores Miami quotes against the Miami band.

With Florida electricity around 15 ¢/kWh, does replacing still pay off in Miami?

Less often than in high-rate metros. At about 15 ¢/kWh, a refrigerator wasting roughly 400 kWh a year costs only around $60/year more to run than a modern one — below the roughly $71 national figure. Cheap power and the lowest labor multiplier we publish both tilt marginal Miami cases toward repairing.
