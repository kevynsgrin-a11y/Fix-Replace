For repair professionals

Let a neutral third party vouch for your quote.

Your honest repair quote competes with a customer’s gut feeling that they’re being upsold. Hand them a neutral, data-backed report that shows repairing is the rational choice — and watch the objection disappear.

The trust gap costs you jobs

You diagnose the fault, quote a fair price, and the customer hesitates — “maybe I should just buy a new one.” They’re not doubting your skill; they’re doubting your incentive. Because you profit from the repair, your reassurance carries less weight than it should. That hesitation is where good jobs stall.

RepairOrReplace closes that gap. Because our verdict is computed by a neutral model from public data — BLS labor rates, EIA energy rates, actuarial lifespans — a report that concludes “repair” is independent corroboration of your quote, not more sales pressure.

The white-label report

Run the customer’s appliance, its age, and your quote through the calculator, and generate a clean, shareable report — a link or a PDF — that lays out the economics in plain English.

Built-in fairness works in your favor

The same guard that protects homeowners protects honest pros. Our model flags a quote as predatory only when it exceeds twice the regional band for that repair. If your pricing is fair — and it almost certainly is — the report simply confirms it sits in range, which is exactly the third-party endorsement a hesitant customer needs.

Independence is the point

To stay credible, the report is neutral by design. It won’t rubber-stamp an inflated quote, and it will happily recommend repair when repair is the rational call. That independence is precisely what makes it persuasive on your behalf.
